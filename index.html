🏆 Our Competitions
⚡ Electronics Technology
Type: Individual
Uniform: Class E — White SkillsUSA polo, black slacks, black closed-toe shoes
Events: CET knowledge test, customer service exam, circuit construction, soldering & troubleshooting
Free Time: Tue 2:00–5:30 PM
Bring: Multimeter, oscilloscope, 2 probes, soldering iron (25W max), hand tools, calculator (non-programmable), resume (PDF)
⚠️ Note: No cell phones allowed in competition area
⚙️ Engineering Technology-Design
Type: Team of 3
Uniform: Class E — White SkillsUSA polo, black slacks, black closed-toe shoes
Events: 10-min oral presentation, design prototype review, engineering notebook, industrial review, on-site problem-solving
Free Time: Tue 3:00–5:30 PM
Bring: Design prototype, design board (max 36"×56" tri-fold), engineering notebook, laptop/tablet (fully charged), industrial review letter, resume (PDF)
⚠️ Note: Prototype must be set up during orientation
📐 Technical Drafting
Type: Individual
Uniform: Class E — White SkillsUSA polo, black slacks, black closed-toe shoes
Events: Technical drafting knowledge test, 3D modeling & 2D drawing extraction (CAD software of choice)
Bring: Laptop with CAD software (offline license!), sci. calculator, mechanical pencil, Machinery's Handbook + 4 refs, 6' surge protector, USB flash drive, resume (PDF)
⚠️ Note: No internet allowed. Submit drawings as PDFs on USB drive.
🎭 Opening & Closing Ceremonies
Type: Team of 7
Uniform: Class A — Official SkillsUSA red blazer, white dress shirt + black tie, black slacks, black closed-toe shoes
Roles: President, VP, Parliamentarian, Reporter, Treasurer, Secretary, Historian
Events: Full opening & closing ceremony script from memory, emblem assembly, pledges in unison
Bring: Resume (PDF), pencil & paper (president brings to orientation)
⚠️ Note: Script must be performed exactly as written — memorized

📣 Need to Know
🏟️
General Sessions — State Farm Arena
Tuesday Opening Session: Keynote speaker is Josef Newgarden — two-time Indy 500 champion and NTT IndyCar Series driver. Don't miss it!

Friday Awards Session: Come-and-go format — check the app for your minute-by-minute recognition time. Be seated in a 100-level/floor seat 15 minutes before your recognition time.

⚠️ Arena Rules: No bags larger than 14"×14"×6". No water bottles or drinks of any kind. For fastest entry use Gates 1 and 2. State Farm Arena is a cashless facility — bring a debit/credit card.
🚇
Getting Around Atlanta — MARTA
MARTA rapid transit runs directly from the airport to downtown. Go to the Domestic Terminal between North & South baggage claims. Take the Red or Gold line to Peachtree Center. Buy a pass at the kiosk. Quick, affordable, easy.

Airport Shuttle: SkillsUSA has partnered with DART transportation for discounted shuttles. Use promo code "SKILLS26" when booking online.
📱
Download the Official NLSC App
Search "NLSC 2025" in the App Store or Google Play. The app has the full schedule, maps, minute-by-minute Awards Session recognition times, Atlanta info, and more. Also check out Expedition NLSC in the app — a digital scavenger hunt where teams of 4 can win daily prizes and a VIP suite + $1,000 grand prize!
🍽️
Food & SkillsUSA Store
Food: 200+ dining options within walking distance. GWCC has budget-friendly options. Both GWCC and State Farm Arena are cashless — bring a card.

SkillsUSA Store (Registration Hall B): Grand Opening Mon 1–6 PM · Tue & Wed 7:30 AM–5 PM · Thu 7:30 AM–2 PM. Glowsticks, apparel, drinkware, backpacks & more. Need competition attire? Get it here!
🎓
SkillsUSA LEARN — Professional Development
Open to both students and educators. Six tracks including Student Development, Student Credentialing, CTE Instructor Innovation, Advisor Development, and credentialing opportunities. Space is limited for credentials — register early!
